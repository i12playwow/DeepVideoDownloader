# Privacy Policy for Deep Grab

Last updated: 2026-09-01

Deep Grab is a browser extension that works together with the Deep Video Downloader
desktop application running on your own computer. This policy explains what data the
extension handles and how it is used.

## What Data We Collect

Deep Grab detects videos that are playing on the page you are viewing. To do this it
reads media addresses (for example `.mp4` and `.m3u8` stream URLs) that the web player
on the page requests. It also reads the current page title so the downloaded file can
be given a useful name.

Deep Grab does **not** collect personal data, health data, financial data, login or
authentication data, personal communications, location, or your browsing history.

## How Data Is Stored

Your extension choices (for example, whether to show only the best-quality video or
whether to send automatically) and the list of videos you have already captured are
stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device only. Nothing is synced to
your Google account.

## How Data Is Used

The detected media address and page title are used only to tell the companion
Deep Video Downloader desktop app which video to download. They are sent over a
local connection on your own computer (at 127.0.0.1) to that app and nowhere else.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, advertising, or
trackers.

## Data Sharing

Deep Grab does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Media addresses
are not sent over the internet; they are delivered only to the local companion
desktop app on the machine you are using.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Settings and the list of already-captured videos are kept in the browser's local
extension storage until you remove the extension. Removing the extension deletes this
locally stored data. Files downloaded by the companion desktop app are saved to the
folder you choose and can be deleted by you at any time.

## Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and note the revision date above.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the publisher using the
support and contact information shown on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
